The Collection

Made to be given.
Hard to give away.

Everything we sell is chosen the same way: we buy it, we play it, and it only makes the shelf if we would happily hand it to someone we like.

Why we exist

Curated,
not collected.

A pro shop wall holds forty balls and tells you nothing. We narrowed it to the twelve that matter, wrote down what each one actually does, and put them in a box worth keeping.

  1. 01

    Chosen, then tested

    Every ball in the pack is a current-model, first-quality retail ball. No refinished, no overruns, no surprises.

  2. 02

    Packaging as the product

    Rigid, foil-stamped, magnet-closed. It opens like something expensive because the presentation is half the gift.

  3. 03

    Information, not marketing

    Compression, cover, layers, spin. Printed on the tray so the recipient can actually learn something.
